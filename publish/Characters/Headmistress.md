This character represents **authoritarianism, tribalism, justice, and hubris**. Domina from Overwatch vibes (image below). 
![[Domina_image.png]]
Very chokehold, iron grasp on social standing...at least to begin with.

Rough arc/development concept:
Start out as an obvious antagonist to the creatives and directly places them in opposition to the Stemmers. (That's the terms ive decided on....for now)
First 'Chapter', she acts as the red herring, an obvious diversion to the actual root of the suicides/evil, the very society they live within. (Some indication she is subservient to externals, such as benefactors who really pull the strings....allegory for oligarchs or patriarchy or historical pressures?)
By 'Chapter' 2, she should be identifiably a red herring, with some indication of a potential redemption. I don't want that,; her story is more fitting if she understands her misgivings, but still receives punitive justice. Whether she dies or replaced is irrelevant, her end should feel tragic and upsetting
Someone who seeks redemption after learning their actions have severely affected those around them, not receiving a good ending may seem unnecessarily cruel, but does an authoritative figure 'deserve' redemption...no. If done correctly, her redemption is the player believing she deserved better, in doing so redeeming her for the player and displaying her legacy might be positive (at least to some individuals, in some way)
TL;DR:
She is bad to start, begins her redemption before being abruptly stopped. This tragic juxtaposition serves a dual purpose: cement the dark narrative by 'chapter' 2, and display the tragedy which comes with redemption. It is possible for anyone, but expecting a 'good' ending is foolish/undeserved.
